Our Founder

About Dr. Yehudah Pryce, DSW, MSW

After serving 16 years of a 24-year prison sentence that began when he was a teenager, Dr. Yehudah Pryce, DSW, LCSW graduated Summa Cum Laude from Adams State University, earned his Master of Social Work from the USC Suzanne Dworak-Peck School of Social Work, where he chaired Unchained Scholars, and was named DSW Student of the Year at Simmons University, where he earned his Doctorate of Social Work.

Today, he practices with Highland Behavioral Health, serves as a psychotherapist with the Chabad Treatment Center’s intensive outpatient program, and supports incarcerated community members preparing for parole through UnCommon Law.
