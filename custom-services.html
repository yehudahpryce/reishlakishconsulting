Reish Lakish Consulting LLC

About Us

Reish Lakish Consulting LLC is committed to client-centered care by designing wellness solutions based on the unique bio-psycho-social-spiritual needs of individuals, families, communities and organizations.

We also provide individual and organizational trainings, and speaker engagement opportunities.

About our founder

Dr. Yehudah Pryce, DSW, LCSW

After serving 16 years of a 24-year prison sentence that began when he was a teenager, Dr. Pryce graduated Summa Cum Laude from Adams State University, earning his Bachelor of Arts in Sociology with an emphasis in Social Welfare.

He subsequently earned his Master of Social Work degree from the USC Suzanne Dworak-Peck School of Social Work, where he was the Chair of Unchained Scholars, and was named DSW Student of the Year at Simmons University, where he earned his Doctorate of Social Work. While incarcerated, he helped develop personal development curriculum and facilitated groups of up to 60 diverse incarcerated community members.

About our founder

The work so far

Dr. Pryce was previously an ICMS (intensive case management services) case manager and program manager serving community members who were chronically homeless and contending with mental health challenges in South Los Angeles and Skid Row.

He went on to serve as a clinical social worker in a collaborative court program for justice-involved transitional age youth with the Orangewood Foundation, and as the Senior Director of National Mental Health & Well-Being programs at Defy Ventures, a national nonprofit that delivers in-prison and reentry curriculum and support.

About our founder

Today

Dr. Pryce is a licensed clinical social worker in private practice with Highland Behavioral Health, serves as a psychotherapist with the Chabad Treatment Center’s intensive outpatient program, and supports incarcerated community members preparing for parole through UnCommon Law.

Dr. Pryce (Yehudah ben Avraham) underwent an Orthodox gerus through the Rabbinical Council of California and lives with his wife and five children in the Pico-Robertson area of Los Angeles, California.

1.

Individual Wellness Consultations

Consultants utilize a lifetime of lived experience of delivering personal development curriculum to incarcerated individuals and individuals transitioning to provide valuable insight into what works to improve wellness.

While this population has special needs that need to be taken into consideration, organizations, adolescents, professionals and everyday working men and women are contending with the vicissitudes of daily life as well.

How it works

A plan built for you

Our consultants conduct extensive assessments to aid in case conceptualization. We then work with our client to deliver a unique, customized personal development plan for them while coaching clients to be the best version of themselves.

Who it’s for

There with you

Whether you’re trying to get the job promotion you want, whether you struggle with substance use, whether you need to be more assertive, less confrontational, or want to approach a potential partner in the most strategic way possible, Reish Lakish consultants will be there with you.

  • Incarcerated individuals
  • Individuals navigating reentry
  • Struggling adolescents
  • Students
  • Professionals
  • Executives
  • Frum youth and adults
  • Community members struggling with addiction

Reish Lakish Consulting offers consulting, coaching and speaking. It is not psychotherapy. For therapy, see Dr. Pryce’s clinical practice at dryehudahprycelcsw.com.

Speaker

Dr. Yehudah Pryce, DSW, MSW

Dr. Yehudah Pryce speaks on his journey from juvenile delinquency and a 24-year prison sentence to Judaism, his lived experience as a convicted street terrorist, and his expertise in smart decarceration.

His experience as a clinical social worker with currently incarcerated and formerly incarcerated community members, as well as with the chronically homeless community members and those struggling with substance use, has enabled Dr. Pryce to pull from several areas of thought and expertise to weave together compelling subject matter that draws Jews closer to Hashem, ahavas Yisroel, and understanding the opportunities of individuals and organizations being able to operate optimally via DEI wellness.
