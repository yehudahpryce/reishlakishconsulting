Individual Wellness Consultations

Consultants utilize a lifetime of lived experience of delivering personal development curriculum to incarcerated individuals and individuals transitioning to provide valuable insight into what works to improve wellness.

While this population has special needs that need to be taken into consideration, organizations, adolescents, professionals and everyday working men and women are contending with the vicissitudes of daily life as well.

Our consultants conduct extensive assessments to aid in case conceptualization. We then work with our client to deliver a unique, customized personal development plan for them while coaching clients to be the best version of themselves.

Whether you’re trying to get the job promotion you want, whether you struggle with substance use, whether you need to be more assertive, less confrontational, or want to approach a potential partner in the most strategic way possible, Reish Lakish consultants will be there with you.
